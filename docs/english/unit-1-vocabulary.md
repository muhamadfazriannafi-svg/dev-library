## Penjelasan Lengkap

### Application (app) <Speak text="application" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — software optimized to perform a specific task such as word processing, accounting, or web browsing.

> *"Examples of popular applications include Microsoft Word, Adobe Photoshop, and Mozilla Firefox."*

<Speak text="Examples of popular applications include Microsoft Word, Adobe Photoshop, and Mozilla Firefox." accents />

**Arti:** perangkat lunak yang dirancang untuk melakukan tugas tertentu, misalnya pengolah kata, akuntansi, atau menjelajah web.

### Component <Speak text="component" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — any device internal to a computer, such as a hard disk drive, a usb port, or a motherboard.

> *"A hardware geek is constantly upgrading components in his computer to achieve more performance."*

<Speak text="A hardware geek is constantly upgrading components in his computer to achieve more performance." accents />

**Arti:** perangkat yang berada di dalam komputer, misalnya hard disk, port USB, atau motherboard.

### Computer <Speak text="computer" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — an electronic device which stores and processes information.

> *"A computer needs to be replaced or upgraded regularly or it will become obsolete."*

<Speak text="A computer needs to be replaced or upgraded regularly or it will become obsolete." accents />

**Arti:** perangkat elektronik yang menyimpan dan memproses informasi.
**Catatan:** *obsolete* = usang / ketinggalan zaman.

### Data <Speak text="data" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — literally meaning 'that which is given', this term refers to raw information of any kind.

> *"The network administrator was fired when he lost all the company data by accidentally formatting the wrong hard disk drive array."*

<Speak text="The network administrator was fired when he lost all the company data by accidentally formatting the wrong hard disk drive array." accents />

**Arti:** informasi mentah dalam bentuk apa pun.
**Catatan:** dalam bahasa Inggris *data* biasanya diperlakukan sebagai kata benda tak terhitung (uncountable). *format* sebagai kata kerja = memformat.

### Database <Speak text="database" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — an organized, electronic collection of information, optimized for fast access, and typically consisting of rows, columns, indexes, and keys.

> *"The international company stored their customer information in a central database in Brussels."*

<Speak text="The international company stored their customer information in a central database in Brussels." accents />

**Arti:** kumpulan informasi elektronik yang terorganisir, dioptimalkan untuk akses cepat, terdiri dari baris, kolom, indeks, dan kunci (keys).

### Hardware <Speak text="hardware" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — physical items which make up a computer system, including: monitors, keyboards, hard drives, and RAM.

> *"Hardware today has become such a commodity that it's often more expensive to repair it than to replace it."*

<Speak text="Hardware today has become such a commodity that it's often more expensive to repair it than to replace it." accents />

**Arti:** benda fisik yang membentuk sistem komputer, misalnya monitor, keyboard, hard drive, dan RAM.
**Catatan:** *commodity* = barang umum yang mudah didapat.

### Internet <Speak text="internet" accents />

**Proper noun (thing)** — the largest known network, connecting millions of computers around the world.

> *"Some people refer to the Internet as an information superhighway."*

<Speak text="Some people refer to the Internet as an information superhighway." accents />

**Arti:** jaringan terbesar yang menghubungkan jutaan komputer di seluruh dunia.
**Catatan:** *information superhighway* = istilah kiasan lama untuk internet.

### Network <Speak text="network" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — a connected group of computers, capable of sharing resources with each other.

> *"The company network consisted of 3 servers, 95 workstations, and 10 printers."*

<Speak text="The company network consisted of 3 servers, 95 workstations, and 10 printers." accents />

**Arti:** sekelompok komputer yang terhubung dan bisa saling berbagi sumber daya.
**Catatan:** *workstation* = komputer kerja.

### Peripheral <Speak text="peripheral" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — an external device or "accessory" which extends the functionality of computer, such as a printer, monitor, microphone, or game controller.

> *"The woman hated the look of all the tangled wires behind her desk, which were caused by so many peripherals."*

<Speak text="The woman hated the look of all the tangled wires behind her desk, which were caused by so many peripherals." accents />

**Arti:** perangkat eksternal atau "aksesori" yang memperluas fungsi komputer, misalnya printer, monitor, mikrofon, atau game controller.
**Catatan:** *tangled wires* = kabel yang kusut.

### Software <Speak text="software" accents />

**Noun (thing)** — an organized set of computer instructions, such as applications (apps), operating systems (OS), or games.

> *"The geek purchased new software for his computer almost every weekend."*

<Speak text="The geek purchased new software for his computer almost every weekend." accents />

**Arti:** kumpulan instruksi terorganisir untuk komputer, misalnya aplikasi (apps), sistem operasi (OS), atau game.
**Catatan:** *software* tidak berjumlah jamak — jangan menulis "softwares".

## Pasangan yang Sering Tertukar

| Istilah | Lawan / Pasangan | Bedanya |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hardware** | **Software** | hardware = benda fisik; software = instruksi/program |
| **Component** | **Peripheral** | component = di **dalam** komputer; peripheral = di **luar** (aksesori) |
| **Data** | **Database** | data = informasi mentah; database = tempat penyimpanan terorganisir |
| **Application** | **Software** | application adalah salah satu **jenis** software |
| **Internet** | **Network** | jaringan global; internet adalah network terbesar |

## Rangkuman Singkat Bacaan

Di unit ini ada bacaan contoh **"Information Technology 101"**. Isinya menceritakan aturan seorang developer saat membangun aplikasi:

1. **Data** aplikasi disimpan di **database** relasional.
2. Pelanggan bisa membeli aplikasi di **Internet**.
3. **Software** harus jalan tanpa **peripheral** tambahan.
4. Program dirancang agar tetap jalan dengan atau tanpa koneksi **network**.
